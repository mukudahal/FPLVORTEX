# v0.9: official squad decision desk

The previous milestone mixed a real roster viewer with a prominent fictional-squad solver and an uncalibrated transfer shortlist. This milestone puts the actual manager's public roster and source facts first, removes that speculative shortlist, and adds constrained single-move feasibility and a facts-based review calendar through GW19.

## Source and synchronization contract

User-initiated `GET /api/v1/fpl/entry?entryId=145784` requests the official FPL host's public entry, history, transfers, catalogue, fixtures, and last-disclosed-GW picks. There are no cookies, credentials, private account routes, transactions, scheduled requests, or automatic refresh. Requests have timeouts, a fixed upstream host, redirect refusal, and validated numeric IDs. The unsupported website interface does not confer a commercial redistribution license; production/source rights review remains outstanding.

Results include per-endpoint URLs, retrieval timestamps, availability/error state and cache age. A bounded process-memory cache holds at most 100 entries per fetch implementation for 60 seconds; identical in-flight requests are coalesced. Expired retrieval failures return failure, never newly timestamped old data. The browser clears the previous decision desk on refresh failure or ID changes. A local freshness timer does not access the network. Transfer checks stop at 15 minutes or the next deadline, whichever comes first.

Profile, history, transfer log, picks, catalogue and fixtures have independent availability states. Rank/points can be unknown while the roster remains usable. Unknown chip history differs from an empty reported history. Fifteen unique official player IDs, fifteen unique slots and distinct captain/vice captain are required. Duplicate catalogue IDs or missing roster mappings suppress planning. Player IDs are never joined to the fictional demo ID namespace.

The current season is derived from the GW1 deadline and must match 2026/27 for planning. Wrong-season or missing fixtures, invalid position totals, incomplete mapping, stale timestamps or a passed target leave facts visible but withhold a plan. A public roster describes the last deadline, not undisclosed changes before the next deadline. An active Free Hit roster suppresses permanent-squad planning until the roster is restored.

## What the desk displays

- Fifteen public picks, captain, vice captain and bench slots, official player IDs, clubs and positions.
- Reported overall rank/points, current GW points, last-deadline bank and team value.
- Reported active chip, past chip use, GW transfer count/cost and public transfer records. Future chip entitlement and current FT count remain unknown.
- Reported season points, goals/assists, starts, minutes, source xG/xA, availability/chance/news and source news timestamps. Source xG/xA are observations, not forecasts.
- Derived ninety-equivalents and minutes per completed/provisionally finished club match. Including provisionally finished fixtures prevents live-GW minutes from being divided by too few matches. In-progress matches and mid-season club moves still limit that denominator.

`appearances=1` is a separate explicit action to request the 15 selected players' element summaries, with at most three requests in flight. It derives starts, sub-on, average minutes per appearance and ninety-equivalents. Minutes alone cannot distinguish an off substitution from a dismissal, so sub-off remains unknown. Failures are shown per player.

## Personalized decision support

`src/official-planner.ts` uses current availability flags and a transparent low-minutes rule (under 60 season minutes per completed club match, at least three such matches, and in the prior XI). These are review priorities with source/editorial confidence labels. No estimated absence duration, projected point advantage or rank probability is inferred. A healthy squad receives a provisional hold/review prompt, not an assertion that holding is optimal. Zero-minute bench reserves are not automatically recommended for replacement.

The calendar covers all upcoming deadlines through GW19, even when there are more than ten weeks remaining. All scheduled fixtures in a GW are retained with official FDR, home/away and opponent. Empty cells mean missing/unassigned coverage or possible blanks, not invented fixtures. Weekly prompts ask the manager to review availability, captaincy, bench cover and chip eligibility. No chip is automatically scheduled from past-use records.

The proposed single-move checker uses official same-position alternatives with available status, no reported next-GW chance below 100%, at least three season starts, and a next-GW fixture. Alternatives are ordered by observed starts then ID, not predicted value. The manager must confirm current bank, actual selling price and free transfers. Feasibility uses integer tenths, correct same-club replacement counting, a maximum of three per club, and the standard four-point cost when no FT is available. It excludes simultaneous moves and chips and does not claim expected gain or recommend execution.

## Speculative and demo boundaries

The v0.8 shrinkage model remains behind an optional “unvalidated research” disclosure. Its XI now respects a legal formation, and multiple fixtures are summed rather than discarded. Incomplete mapping suppresses lineup/captain output. The fictional ten-week transfer/chip solver is preserved in separately labelled expandable sections and its dedicated page. Manual chip controls apply to that demo only.

No player-level prospective validation, top-5K probability model, FPLReview/Opta equivalence, exact real multistep transfer/chip optimizer or learned forecast is delivered. The top-5K by GW19 target remains aspirational.

## Validation

Deterministic regression tests cover official mapping, IDs/captains/slots, negative/zero facts, source failures, unknown rank, wrong season, stale/deadline boundaries, duplicates, doubles/missing fixtures, legal XIs, provisional match denominators, transfer economics/club limits/hits, cache expiry/coalescing, opt-in appearance concurrency, unknown sub-off and HTML escaping. Test identities are synthetic. CI uses the committed lockfile, typecheck/build/tests and its PostgreSQL service. No manager snapshots or diagnostic credentials are committed.

Local live verification confirmed 15 mappings, GW8–19 coverage and 15 available element summaries from official endpoints on October 17, 2026. This demonstrates connectivity and data handling, not prediction accuracy. Browser checks verify the actual roster, source separation, transfer guard behavior, responsive layout and retained demo solver. See the PR for exact final test counts and CI status.
